What PreQuit is

A social platform that knows the difference between loud and useful.

Every post belongs to topics. Every interaction you have — what you publish, like, save, share and reply to — folds into an interest profile that decays over time, so what you cared about last year stops crowding out what you care about now. That profile is what the feed ranks against, and what search reasons over.

Interest-ranked

Candidates are scored on topic affinity, semantic similarity, quality and recency — then diversified so one voice cannot take the page.

Explains itself

Every post carries the reason it reached you, straight from the ranker. No silent algorithm.

Mutuals only

A direct message channel opens when two people follow each other. Nobody can slide into your inbox uninvited.

No ads

Nothing in the ranking is for sale, so relevance is the only thing the feed is optimising for.

The feed

Ranked on what you are into, not who you follow.

Following someone is a weak signal — people post about more than one thing. PreQuit draws candidates from the people you follow, the topics you lean into and from users whose interests sit close to yours, then ranks the pool as one.

  • Topic affinity and semantic closeness to your interest profile
  • A guaranteed share of the page from people you actually follow
  • Deliberate exploration, so the feed cannot collapse into one subject
  • Diversity caps, so no single author dominates a session
  • Posts you have already seen are held back automatically

Search

Describe the problem. Meet the people who fixed it.

Type a sentence rather than a keyword. PreQuit reads the intent, retrieves both lexically and semantically, fuses the two rankings, and answers with the posts that address it — plus the people who have demonstrated real competence in that topic.

  • Expertise is earned from evidence, not claimed in a bio
  • Measured from published work, answers marked helpful and sustained engagement
  • Inactivity decays a score, so the list stays current
  • Thin evidence is labelled emerging rather than ranked as proven

Messages

A channel that opens when both people agree.

There is no request inbox to triage and no stranger in your messages. Follow each other and the conversation exists; stop following and it closes. Blocks are enforced on the way in, not after the fact.

  • Mutual follow is the only key that opens a conversation
  • Messages arrive live, and send optimistically so the app never feels slow
  • A failed send is marked, never silently dropped
  • Share a post straight into a thread

Under it

Built to stay fast when it is under pressure.

Ranking a feed per request is expensive. The parts that make that affordable are the parts you are never supposed to notice.

It balances itself

Work is spread across every core and each process decides for itself how much it can run at once, watching its own latency to find the limit rather than guessing at one.

Reads never starve writes

Signing in, posting and browsing each hold a reserved share of capacity, so one slow query cannot crowd out everything else. At the edge, requests are turned away quickly rather than queued until they time out.

Nothing is lost on a crash

A like and the notification it causes are committed together. If a process dies between the two, the work is still queued and runs when it comes back.
